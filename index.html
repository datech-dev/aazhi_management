Frequently Asked Questions

What happens in the Pro Plan AI Assistant & Credits?
In the Pro Plan (₹1,299/mo), you get our AI Assistant that automatically connects to your WhatsApp. It interacts with your customers, checks order status, shares receipts, and books trial dates. Every month, you receive ₹2,000 worth of AI credits included free to cover all WhatsApp AI messages!
How does the Automated Instagram Weekly Backup & Recovery work?
Instagram is the #1 sales engine for boutiques. If your account gets compromised, restricted, or deleted, our automated weekly scheduler captures a full snapshot of your posts, media links, captions, bio, and engagement metrics directly to your private database. You can restore or export your entire catalog in just 1 click anytime!
Can my Master Tailors use Aazhi easily on mobile phones?
Yes! Aazhi Studio is 100% mobile responsive. Your master tailors can open the visual Kanban board on any Android phone, iPhone, or tablet to view cutting/stitching instructions and upload fabric photos.
What if I exceed 100 orders on the Basic Plan?
If your boutique grows past 100 orders, you can upgrade to the Premium Plan (₹699/mo) with 1-click. Premium gives you unlimited customer profiles, unlimited orders, and unlimited staff logins.
Is my boutique data safe and private?
Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade cloud encryption with daily automated backups. Your customer measurements and sales figures belong strictly to your business.
